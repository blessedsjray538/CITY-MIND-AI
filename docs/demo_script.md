# CITYMIND AI — Hackathon Demonstration Script

This script walks through the 4 live demonstration scenarios defined in Section 24 of the CITYMIND AI Master Specification.

---

## 🎬 Opening: The Cinematic Activation
1. Launch the application: `http://localhost:5173`.
2. Observe the dark opening screen.
3. Point out the glowing tracer light outlining the letters: **CITYMIND AI**.
4. Highlight the tagline reveal: *"AN INTELLIGENT DIGITAL TWIN OF A CITY (Bhubaneswar, Odisha)"*.
5. Click **ENTER COMMAND ENVIRONMENT** (or press `[ESC]` to skip).
6. Point out the perimeter scanning transition beam as the interface initializes.

---

## 🚗 Scenario 1: Traffic Intelligence & What-If Rerouting
1. Navigate to **Traffic Intelligence** via the left sidebar.
2. Note that the entire viewport transforms into a dedicated traffic command center.
3. Show the interactive map of the **Jaydev Vihar – Acharya Vihar – Vani Vihar** corridor with color-coded flow (green/yellow/red).
4. Click **CLOSE JAYDEV VIHAR ROAD** in the right console:
   - Observe the road status turn to **Severe / Closed (Red)**.
   - Point out the traffic spillover to Nandankanan Expressway and Sainik School Road.
   - Observe the congestion level jump from 48.2% to 74.8%.
5. Click **OPTIMIZE SIGNAL TIMINGS**:
   - Observe the before-and-after signal cycle calculation (75s → 58s).
   - Point out the estimated **38.1% waiting time reduction**.
6. Open the right-side **AI Assistant** and click:
   *"What will happen if Jaydev Vihar flyover is closed?"*
   - Show the AI synthesizing the live simulation state with exact figures.

---

## 🌪️ Scenario 2: Weather Telemetry & Disaster Early Warning
1. Navigate to **Weather Intelligence**:
   - Demonstrate the live meteorological station observation for Bhubaneswar.
   - Highlight current temperature, wind speed/direction, humidity, and barometric pressure.
   - Toggle through the meteorological layers: **Wind Streamlines**, **Thermal Gradient**, and **Doppler Radar**.
2. Navigate to **Disaster Intelligence**:
   - Highlight the active **Bay of Bengal Monsoon Surge Advisory**.
   - Open the **Odisha Historical Disaster Knowledge Archive**.
   - Select the **1999 Odisha Super Cyclone (05B)**:
     - Point out the verified historical statistics: **260 km/h wind speed**, **912 hPa central pressure**, **9,887 official casualties**, and the establishment of **OSDMA**.
   - Review the **Cyclone Comparative Engine** comparing the current low-pressure system against the 1999 benchmark.
3. Ask the AI:
   *"Tell me about the 1999 Odisha Super Cyclone."*
   - Show the AI attributing verified historical archives with zero hallucination.

---

## ♻️ Scenario 3: Smart Waste Red/Green Geospatial Workflow
1. Navigate to **Waste Management**:
   - Point out the dark municipal map with existing incidents.
   - Explain the rule: **RED = Waste Reported (Cleaning Required)**, **GREEN = Cleaned & Verified**.
2. Click **REPORT NEW WASTE INCIDENT**:
   - Title: *"Overflowing plastic accumulation near Jaydev Vihar underpass"*.
   - Location: Jaydev Vihar Square.
   - Category: Plastic.
   - Priority: High.
   - Click **SUBMIT REPORT**.
   - **Observe the new incident appear in RED on the map and in the feed!**
3. Select the reported incident in the inspector panel and click **MARK AS CLEANED**:
   - **Observe the marker dynamically transform from RED to GREEN!**
   - Note the updated resolution rate and completion timestamp.
4. Point out the **Dijkstra Optimized Collection Route** for BMC compactor truck OD-02-AT-4481.

## 🧠 Scenario 4: Cross-Department Central AI Synthesis
1. Click the **AI ASSISTANT** button on the top bar from any department.
2. Ask:
   *"How could heavy rainfall affect traffic and waste management in Bhubaneswar?"*
3. Show the AI synthesizing across three distinct departments:
   - **Atmospheric:** Current coastal humidity and rain probabilities.
   - **Traffic:** Water pooling risk at Jaydev Vihar underpass reducing speeds to 18 km/h.
   - **Waste:** Plastic obstruction at stormwater gratings aggravating flooding.
4. Highlight the suggested cross-departmental actionable interventions.
